_Requested by **Guy**_

## Summary

Guy clicked the Flips icon on another stash tab and got "Scarab tab only", and asked why the click is not aware of which tab is open.

- **Which tab:** the reader now also recognises the Delirium, Delve, Essence and Blight tabs, each by a fixed ornament in the tab that holds no items.
- **Prices:** each tab uses its own family's prices and the flip method picked for that family in the panel, with the same filters as the panel. Stack totals use that method's recipe size, so a Harvest reroll counts every item.
- **Full slots:** on the new tabs a slot counts as full only if its stack count reads, as their backings vary too much for the Scarab tab's blueness test. Ghost slots got no count on the shots checked.
- **Notes:** an unsupported tab's note now lists the tabs that work. A missing price says which: "No orb prices yet", or "No Primal lifeforce price yet".
- **Speed:** about 100 to 240 ms a read in tests, excluding the screenshot, because each tab's search is limited to near its usual spot.

## Limits

- Catalysts and Runegrafts are not covered: I have no confirmed slot map for them yet.
- The new tabs read only at a 1440p game window. Any other size says the tab can't be read.
- Essence defaults to Harvest, which prices only Deafening, so lower tiers get outlines only when the panel's Essences method is set to Vendor up a tier.
- A tab's picture must stay within 120 px across and 130 px down of where it was on Guy's shots. Further than that reads as unknown.
- Fossil, Delirium and Essence names come from icon matching, spot-checked by eye on montages, not by hovering each item. Oil names come from the tier order Guy's hovers confirmed. A swapped name would give a wrong figure with no warning.
- The digit shapes come from Scarab tab shots. They read all the new tabs correctly, including an older Essence shot used only as a check, but a digit dimmed by the cursor or an effect ends the number early, so 524 can read as 52 and show too low a total. This now also applies to the Scarab tab: a trailing bright item can't otherwise be told apart from a dim digit.
- Kalguuran, Primal and Challenging Delirium Orbs have no reroll weights, so they never get an outline, with no note saying why.
- The Delve and Essence tabs have no price fixtures, so tests show the tab is found and its counts read, not outlines drawn on it.
- Not checked in the real app.
