Guy picked B + X1 (as a setting) + X2 + T2 (on demand only) from the [mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/9sQtqoiMd4NhZafwQ2vsBk).

## What's in it
- **Disenchant panel** (new Economy module `disenchant`, widget `woke-disenchant`)
  - Ranks every unique that has a dust value by dust per chaos, at item level 84:
    - weapons and armour at 20% quality
    - jewellery catalysed (20 of the cheapest catalyst) only when that gives more dust per chaos
    - quivers and always-corrupted uniques at 0% quality
  - Four rankings: dust per chaos, including the gold fee (at your gold value), per inventory slot, and per gold.
  - Filters for least dust, highest price, highest gold fee, Hide thin, and a text search.
  - Trade links with an item level floor (84 by default, set in Settings).
  - Ticks for bought items, saved per league, with a button to clear them.
- **Listing counts (T2):** fetched only when you press the button. That makes three poe.ninja requests, then the button is off for 5 minutes. Counts never refresh on their own.
- **Price check (X1):** an optional "Disenchant · N dust/c" chip for uniques, off by default. It's switched on, with its dust-per-chaos line, in Settings > Price check.
- **Loot filter preset (X2):**
  - Switches on NeverSink's three `exdust` tiers, which ship commented out.
  - Optionally adds a rule built from today's prices: bases where every unique gives at least the line in dust per chaos, for drops of item level 82 or higher.
  - The rule leaves out replicas and Foulborn drops, and skips any base with a valuable, thin, unpriced or dust-less unique.
- **No new poller:** prices come from the poe.ninja download the app already makes, read through the price index while the panel is open.
- **New league:** the league comes from Settings, and dust values come with the item data. The footer counts priced uniques that have no dust number yet.
- Credited in Settings > Credits, and the README module table is updated.

## Review
I did an adversarial review and fixed what it found:
- A Foulborn price could make a valuable unique on a base look cheap.
- Uniques with no dust number could share a base with the rule and get its styling.
- The count button could be spammed.
- Missing counts showed as a red 0.
- I replaced a ticking clock with a one-shot timer.
