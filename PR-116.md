Builds the Prices tab and dock card from the mocks Guy approved (`Prices option 1`, filter health, dock card).

**Valuable items your filter hides** (was "Filter health")
- Rows name the item behind the price, and count the trash that comes with it ("Kingmaker, highest priced of 2, 1 trash").
- Opening a row lists every unique on the base with its icon and a price bar: gold at or above the safety net, plain in between, red under 5c.
- It says what "Show it" will change in the file, checked in a test against what the edit really does.
- "Not for me" clears a row until its price doubles.

**Prices**
- The apply bar stays pinned on top and always shows the state. "Apply N and save" saves and reloads too.
- Group and tier names link into Rules with that item picked, and Back returns to Prices.
- Not moved rows get "Move anyway" (thin market) or "Unlock" (locked).
- The Changes tab counts price moves, so 208 moved items no longer read as 0 changes.

**Dock card**
- "See it" opens the right tab. "Got it" holds until the number changes. "Don't tell me about this" turns the notice off, with a switch in Prices to turn it back on.
- Warnings that the filter isn't working can't be turned off, and their Got it lasts until a restart.

**Tests:** `filter-health.test.ts`, and a new e2e spec that opens a hidden unique, follows the rule link and Back, and checks the saved file after Show it. The poe.ninja fixture gains two uniques. The dock buttons have unit tests for their logic but no e2e.
