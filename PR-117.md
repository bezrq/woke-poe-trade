_Requested by **Guy**_

The campaign tracker becomes only a dock icon and hover card, and goes quiet on characters past the campaign. Guy's picks from the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/6tMUj9A4ss2A1RZLxsAhdr): B (live icon) and P1 (pinned values in the icon).

## What changes
- **Panel removed:** no strip or guide panel, also with the overlay open. A click on the icon with the overlay open keeps the card open and adds the step buttons, splits, the character switch and the full build.
- **Live icon:** a ring shows act progress. The badge comes from the route step, so it can't disagree with the steps (the A1 vs Act 4 bug). It only grows into a line when the next step changes.
- **Sleep:** entering a map puts the route to sleep, so there's no icon and towns stop walking it forward. A campaign zone that isn't a town wakes it. Client.txt doesn't name the character, so where you go decides.
- **Pins:** off by default. "Pin to screen" on the card opens settings, where run, act, ahead or behind, next boss, zone, level and step can widen the icon.
- **Run timer:** it docks only while the campaign icon shows, and stays quiet once the route sleeps.

## Known limits
- A parked levelling character's route stays asleep until it enters a non-town campaign zone.
- Past runs can only be reached from the card while the route is awake, unless the splits hotkey is set.
- After "Pin to screen" opens settings from play, the overlay stays open until the overlay key.
