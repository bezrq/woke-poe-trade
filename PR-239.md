_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy picked layout A from [the mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/J2d63jbZGnic74kadKWzrB). Closed rows in the trade card show their age at rest, and Reopen and × take its place on hover or keyboard focus.

× removes the row, with an Undo that covers every row removed in the last 5 seconds.

**Staying removed:** at startup the app rereads the last 30 minutes of Client.txt, and the history record is what keeps a closed offer closed. So a removal inside that window leaves a hidden record, which is deleted at the first load after the window passes. Older removals are deleted right away. A newer whisper from the same player still opens a new offer.

**History ages:** closed one-line rows now show an age, and the clock only ran while offers were open, so it would have frozen. It now also ticks while History is open and on screen, and not while you play.

**Known limit:** with two Trades widgets, each keeps its own offers, so × in one doesn't remove the copy in the other.

- Adversarial review ran, and its findings are fixed apart from the limit above.
