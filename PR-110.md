_Requested by **Guy**_

Bugs 101-110 of the bug hunt.

- **101. Trade: item names with " in " lost their price.** "A Note in the Wind listed for 5 chaos" read as the item "A Note", no price, league "the Wind listed for…".
- **102. Trade: skip did nothing on a buyer in your area.** They stayed on top of anyone who wasn't there, so the step key kept acting on them.
- **103. Trade: skip outlived new activity.** A skipped buyer who joined or wrote again stayed behind the others.
- **104. Trade: a repeat whisper at a new price kept the old one.** The offer and the sales log showed the first price.
- **105. Map scan: stopped at once after time away.** After 3 minutes alt-tabbed, or with the cursor off the game, the next frame ended it with "nothing to read". Only time on the game counts now.
- **106. Map scan: two- and three-line map mods were never matched.** OCR reads one line at a time, so a rating on one of those mods did nothing, and its lines could read as unsure. Blight variants that differ only in a fixed number stay apart.
- **107. Map mods: copying a profile lost settings.** "Any minimum" and auto-trim weren't copied.
- **108. Market: moves in divines fell back to chaos** when the baseline came from poe.ninja's trend, so a divine price swing looked like every item moving.
- **109. Regex library: a plain string starting with `[` failed to import.** It was read as broken JSON.
- **110. Investments: one unusable league stopped an import.** Later leagues were skipped, and ones already saved didn't show until restart.
