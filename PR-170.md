_Requested by **Guy**_

Replaying a real Client.txt (58,545 events) through master gave a 2.2-minute PB, zero-length acts in 12 of 24 finished runs, and no boss mark at all, because boss arenas never write a log line. After this change the same log gives a 427-minute PB, no zero-length acts, and all 18 boss marks on most finished runs.

- A jump of more than one act is treated as another character, so the run stays put. It catches up when its own character levels up in the later act, for lines missed while the app was closed.
- A run started by hand begins in the act you're in.
- Runs saved with an act under 30 seconds stay out of PB and sum of best, and the PB no longer drops off the 30-run history.
- Bosses are timed by their first line in their own area, or by entering their area if they stay silent.
- Invented lines in `campaign-act1.txt` are replaced with real ones.

**Known limit:** an alt entering exactly the next act still counts as progress, including an endgame character at the Epilogue when the run is in act 10. The log can't tell them apart. Per-character state is WOKEPOET-90.

Closes WOKEPOET-84, WOKEPOET-85, WOKEPOET-86.
