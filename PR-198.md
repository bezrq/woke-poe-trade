_Requested by **Guy**_

## Summary

Adds on-demand outlines to the Scarab stash tab, as in mock [2b](https://claude.ai/artifact/HZ3QdC95eYt4ZZY6CPyygs): pale to bright green by profit per scarab, with the profit written on the slot, and amber near break-even.

- **On demand:** a new hotkey in Flips settings, unset by default. Nothing reads the screen until it's pressed.
- **Fast:** one screenshot and plain pixel sums, with no OCR worker. The read took about 45 ms on the real screenshot in tests, not counting taking the screenshot itself. `Stash outlines: read` is recorded in the perf log for the real number.
- **Clears easily:** on any click, scroll or non-modifier key, a zone change, opening the overlay, or after 60 seconds, so outlines can't sit on the wrong slots after a tab swap. The hotkey also toggles them off. The watcher only listens while outlines show.
- **Panel position:** the SCARAB button text is searched for on each press and slots are placed from it, and a symbol only that tab has confirms it. If either is missing it draws nothing and shows a short note on the dock icon.

## How the slot table was made

`main/src/woke/stash-scarab-data.ts` is generated by `testing/tools/make-stash-slots.py` from Guy's screenshot, by matching each slot against the game's item icons, with one family per row and no name used twice. It names 115 slots, spot-checked by eye rather than against a full list, so a wrong name would give a wrong outline.

Limits:
- Scarab tab only.
- Tested on real screenshots at 2560x1440. Moved panels and 720p are simulated from the same image, so Faustus and real smaller windows are untested.
- Stack counts are not read yet, so there are no per-stack totals.
- A game tooltip over the tab hides what's under it: slots under it may lose their outline, and if it covers the SCARAB text or row symbol the read says it can't read the tab. The settings hint says to keep the mouse off the tab. Untested on a real tooltip.
- Six scarabs with no weight (all four Trarthan, Abyss of Crystals, Abyss of the Consort) never get an outline.
- Untraded prices and rare-and-cheap scarabs get no outline, matching the panel's advice.
- Not checked in the real app or in game. Whether GGG's rules allow this was not checked; it reads the screen and presses nothing.

## Testing
