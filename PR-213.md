_Requested by **Guy**_

Links could go to 7. The number tiles in the price check now stop at game limits, and dragging is easier to control.

- **Limits**, typed, scrolled or dragged: links 6, white sockets 6, map tier 17, area level 86, item level 100, gem level 21, quality 30. Stock and sentinel charge stay uncapped. An item above a limit keeps its value.
- **Slower drag**: 20 px a step, was 8.
- **Drag from the number**: pressing a number no longer focuses it straight away, so a drag can start there. A plain click opens it for typing on release. Text-drag on a selected number is blocked, since that cancelled the pointer mid-drag.

Tests: `testing/e2e/number-tiles.spec.ts` covers all of the above with real mouse input; the existing tile test moved to the new drag speed.
