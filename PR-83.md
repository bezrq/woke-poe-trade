### Window controls on panel top bars, minimize and right-click menu

**Panels now have settings, move, delete, minimize and hide buttons on the title bar, and the hover corner menu is gone.**

- Right-click on any widget gives the same options plus Reset size. Rows with their own menu keep it.
- Minimize collapses a panel to a short title bar and hides its secondary panels. Contents stay mounted, so a live search keeps running. Double-click the title to toggle.
- The panel returns to the same place unless the bar was moved; then it opens toward the free side of the screen.
- Reset size moved from double-click to the right-click menu on main panels.
- Delete acts at once with a 10 s Undo instead of hold-to-delete.
- Widgets without a title bar (campaign strip, run timer, stash/item search, image strip, delve grid) get the options by right-click only.

Validated: 160 tests, vue-tsc, eslint; in the browser with Guy's saved layout (read-only copy): minimize, drag while minimized, restore on screen, right-click menu, delete + undo. Not run in the Electron overlay. Expect small merge conflicts with WOKEPOET-10 in `Widget.vue` and `WPanel.vue`.
