### Keep panel resize grips reachable: all-edge resize, clamp to screen

**Panels now resize from every edge and corner, and a widget that overflows is drawn back on screen.**

Root cause of the unreachable grip: the panel's CSS zoom multiplies vh and vw too, so a 75vh panel is taller than the screen at 135% UI scale (measured: 50vh under zoom 1.5 is 75% of the window).

- Sizes given in vh and vw now mean the real share of the window, so large panels get smaller at UI scales above 100%.
- Resizing keeps the far edge in place by moving the anchor. Widgets that can't move keep only the sides away from their anchor.
- Height is capped by the room from the anchor on every anchor side, with a 240px floor.
- The on-screen shift isn't saved until the user moves or resizes the widget, so a briefly small overlay window can't lose the layout. A big panel dragged partly off screen stays there until it's next opened.

**Validation:** 264 vitest tests, eslint, vue-tsc. A throwaway harness at 135% scale in Chromium/Edge covered every handle across 5 anchors, two-panel widgets, window shrink and regrow, and reopening. Not yet tested in the real overlay.
