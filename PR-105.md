### Keep panel resize grips reachable: all-edge resize, clamp to screen

**Panels now resize from every edge and corner, and overflowing widgets are drawn back on screen.** Root cause of the unreachable grip: the panel's CSS zoom multiplies vh/vw, so a 75vh panel is taller than the screen at 135% UI scale (measured: 50vh under zoom 1.5 is 75% of the window). Validated: 264 vitest tests, eslint, vue-tsc; a harness at 135% in Chromium/Edge for all handles across 5 anchors, two-panel widgets, window shrink and regrow, reopen. Not tested in the real overlay yet. README updated.
