_Requested by **Guy**_

## What
Adds a **Skip thin** chip to the Lab gems window, saved per window and off by default (option A from the mocks). When on, thin-market gems count as 0 and leave the lists.

It drives the colour pools, the Bring table, runner profit, the in-lab dock card and the Specific gem tab. Pools show "N thin left out" in place of "each X%".

| Level 1, live prices | Now | Skip thin |
|---|---|---|
| Red | 254c | 106c |
| Green | 245c | 113c |
| Blue | 1,314c | 71c |

## Decisions
- Thin gems stay in the pool at 0, because the font can still offer them. EV is therefore a floor.
- Base gem prices are never dropped: an unknown base counts as free in `pickProfit`, which would inflate profit.
