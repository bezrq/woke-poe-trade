_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy asked whether the Essence flip counts special essences. It doesn't: Hysteria, Insanity, Horror and Delirium never reach either flip, and a new test pins that.

Checking that turned up the opposite problem: **Essence of Doubt was missing.**

- PoeData.dev's essence sample lists Deafening Doubt as its input, so Doubt never came up as a result and has no weight.
- The upgrade chains are built from the weight list, so every Doubt "Vendor up a tier" row was missing.
- Deafening Doubt also sat out of the reroll average.

Doubt now takes the average of the 19 sampled weights. That's an estimate, and the source note says so.

**Checks:** vitest (2440 pass), `typecheck:test`, lint and build all ran locally. Two of the new tests fail without the fix. The change wasn't checked in the running overlay, because Guy was in the game.
