Guy asked for the demo to show the rail tools working, a price check that shows off its features, and the hover icons in context (a boss arena showing drops, the campaign showing progress).

## What changes

- **New scenes**: Hideout trade, Boss arena and Campaign.
  - Each one replays recorded Client.txt lines, so the icon row along the top shows what fits the zone:
    - Trades: two buyers whisper.
    - Boss runs: an Uber Atziri arena, with drops and prices.
    - Campaign: act 1 with next steps and the run timer.
  - A marker points at the icon to rest the mouse on. It moves with the icon and hides while the card is open.
- **Rail tools have data**:
  - Session analytics and the Trades sales log get sample history from `docs/public/demo/seed.json`. Its times are relative to the visit, so the data never goes stale.
  - Boss drops and session loot get sample prices from `docs/scripts/demo-prices.json`, which is merged into the poe.ninja fixture at build time only, so tests are unaffected.
- **Price check**: the hint under the dock explains what each sample item shows: likely price and range, roll presets, the instant poe.ninja price, and the map danger strip.
- **Fixes in the demo shim**:
  - Tools that open themselves on first use are closed again before the visitor sees them.
  - Zone replays no longer carry the app's `config-changed` echo, which reopened them.
  - The latest prices come from what the overlay collects in the demo, or from http.json, instead of a stale recorded answer.
- **Recorder**: `demo-record.spec.ts` now records the four zone scenes, and adds boss runs, campaign and run timer to the rail.
