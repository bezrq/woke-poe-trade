### Make the PoB builds panel look like Path of Exile

**The build panel now looks like the game.** Gem names take their attribute colour with art on Leveling, Gear and Setups. Gear shows real item icons in inventory order. Hovering an item or gem shows a game-style tooltip. Notes keep PoB's colour codes. Filter tab unchanged.

Key files: `renderer/src/web/woke/pob-builds/` (`GameTooltip.vue`, `GemName.vue`, `item-art.ts`, `tooltip.ts`, `BuildDetail.vue`), `main/src/woke/pob-parse.ts`.

Validated: vue-tsc and eslint clean, 166 renderer tests pass, new `testing/e2e/pob-builds.spec.ts` passes on the fixture and on Guy's real builds at 1080p and 1440p. Not run against latest master: local `git fetch` fails on a crash-corrupted ref (`refs/heads/flux/WOKEPOET-11-...`, all NUL bytes, its PR #83 is merged). CI needs to cover the merged result.

Known gap: 10 of the 278 gems in Guy's builds have no colour data, mostly item-granted skills, and show white. Docs: UI README updated with the `w-gem-*` classes.
