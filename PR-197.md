_Requested by **Guy**_

## What

Guy's filter editor showed the Save + reload button on top of the "Add an item" box, with the panel cut off below it. The columns had a fixed height of `min(36rem, 70vh)`, and that ignored the panel's real height. When the panel was shorter, whether zoomed by the UI scale or resized by hand, the columns ran on under the footer.

- `WPanel` gets a `fill` prop: the body hands its height to its children.
- The Rules layout now shrinks and grows with the panel, and each column scrolls.
- All the editor's tab heights divide `vh` by `--w-ui-scale`, the way the panel's own max height does.

## Checks

Typecheck, lint and build pass. All 17 loot filter and item-tip e2e specs pass, including a new one: at panel heights of 320, 450 and 560 the box stays above the footer, with nothing covering it. Before the fix, at the same heights, the box sat at y 781 with Save at y 422.

I forced those heights in the test. I didn't reproduce Guy's actual UI scale.
