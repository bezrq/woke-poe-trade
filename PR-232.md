_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy's pick from [the mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/5DWE47hNrTyG3iW4z4CocH): Mark B and Override B, with updates bundled in the app.

- **Scarabs:** raw counts from PoeData.dev's two 3.28 datasets, 22,431 rolls. Its calculated `bayesian.json` disagrees with those counts (Divination Scarab of Plenty: 0.07% against about 0.5%), so it isn't used. The wiki tier fallback goes, as the 3.28 data measures all of those scarabs.
- **Estimates:** Delirium, scarabs and runegrafts with no measured weight take the average of the listed weights and show an EST chip. Doubt is marked too. Catalysts still leave theirs out.
- **Your own weights:** a Drop weights list in the Flips settings, by family, typed as % of results. The footer's Weights link goes there. Overrides are saved apart from the data, so updates don't touch them. 0 means it never comes up as a result.
- **Removed:** Foreboding and Amorphous orbs and Perfect Fossil, which aren't in the game data and inflated the "unpriced weight" warning.

Limit: an estimate on an expensive item can move a tab's EV a lot, and these items may not come from the recipe at all (Trarthan scarabs are absent from all 22,431 rolls). The chip and the footer say so, and 0 takes one out.

Tested: type check, lint, 2641 unit tests, `scarab-flip.spec.ts` (16 tests, new one covers the chip and an override). Part of WOKEPOET-134.
