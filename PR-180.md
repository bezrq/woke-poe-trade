_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy asked for the whole top section of the price check to collapse into a single row that opens on demand, so the item, mods and listings come first.

- Collapsed, the row shows the price or "Not worth listing", the confidence badge, the cheapest listing, poe.ninja's price and the chevron.
- A warning that changes what the price means is a small flag on the row. Its tooltip has the text, and clicking it opens the section. Fixing risk keeps a short label, such as "Fixing: medium".
- Everything else opens with the chevron: warning text, copy buttons, the range, the trusted count, the sparkline and chart, the divine rate, the sources and the poeprices toggle.

The copy buttons now take one extra click. I left them behind the chevron because Guy asked for the entire section.
