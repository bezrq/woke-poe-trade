_Requested by **Guy**_

Small bugs from the price check critique.

- What drives the price now treats total life and total energy shield as valuable. The seed list said "to maximum Life", which the "total maximum Life" stat doesn't contain, so life could miss the 3 tested mods.
- If the second page of listings fails, the first page's rows stay on screen and no further pages load. It used to replace them with an error box.
- A bulk search that's been replaced can no longer show its error or change the newer one's totals.
- Base percentile no longer starts ticked on uniques, where a perfect-base Kaom's Heart searched only perfect bases.
- A small refresh button in the listings toolbar forces a new search, clearing kept results and measured drivers. Results are otherwise kept for 5 minutes. If the rate limit would block it, it says how long to wait and leaves your results alone.

Price flooring from the same list is fixed in #139.

Builds on #139, so this PR targets that branch.

Checks: adversarial review, then fixes for what it found. New tests, full unit suite, typecheck, lint and build. The page failure, bulk and refresh fixes are in Vue components with no test covering them, and I didn't open the price check window, so the button's look is unchecked.
