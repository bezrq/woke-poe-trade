_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy picked two of the mocked views and asked for a quick way to switch between them, such as the scroll wheel.

- **Numbers:** each number on a mod line is white and bolder. The roll % is red under 34, yellow to 66, green from 67, and a gold block at 100.
- **Two views:** the range in the line, `136(120-139)%` in tan (default), or as a bar per rolled number with its % on the bar. The bars view has no % column, which saves width, and each bar is filled and coloured by its own roll.
- **Switching:** Shift + scroll over a listing with a tooltip switches and saves. Plain scroll still scrolls the results. A flick counts once, because only a 200 ms pause re-arms it. Price check settings have the same choice.

Adversarial review findings fixed: a long flick flipping views repeatedly, and rows with no tooltip changing the setting.
