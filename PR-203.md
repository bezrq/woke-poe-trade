## Summary

```diff
 Flips buy list (default sort: Profit each, highest first)
-  Scarab | Price | Profit each | Market / day | Max profit / day
+  Scarab ^ | Price ^ | Profit each ^ | Supply ^      (^ = click to sort, click again to flip)
```

- Supply is a word from poe.ninja's daily volume: High from 100 a day, Medium from 20, Low below, None with no trades. There is one scale for every tab. On Allflame's feed (5 Nov), scarabs run from Low to High and no runegraft reaches Medium.
- Oil upgrades grade the thinner side of the recipe, because a recipe needs buyers for the result too.
- Supply sort goes by word, with the best profit first inside each word.
- The picked sort is saved per panel. Bad saved values fall back to Profit each.
- The Rare & cheap tab also swaps Market / day for Supply, but its headers don't sort.
- The volume data stays. `buyList` keeps its order, so the dock card and stash outlines don't change.
