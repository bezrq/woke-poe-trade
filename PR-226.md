Options A and B from the mocks.

**Swap check:** the price check gets a "Compare to your boots" chip on gear. On hover it lists the numbers against the item your build wears, using your saved over-cap resistances. It reports "under your cap" only when a capped resistance really falls below it. It only says better or worse when nothing else differs, since other mods need PoB's calculation; otherwise it says what changed and that the other mods differ. Rings go against the one you lose least by replacing.

**Build hover icon:** the build in use gets a dock icon. Hovering it shows links, the next tree, pantheon and saved stats, including in maps. A click opens the panel.

Saved stats only feed the comparison when the set in use is the set PoB saved on.
