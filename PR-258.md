Pick **C** from the [mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/Hu3xfc2xLNn4EFQgMyBdmR), made bigger: right-clicking any item that shows a game tooltip opens a menu with the item's name, art and price at the top, then a strip of big buttons:

- **Price**: opens the price check (uniques, gems, cards, currency/scarabs/essences/tattoos; build gear and trade listings with their full text)
- **Trade**: trade site search in your league
- **Wiki**: the in-overlay item info panel (wiki/poedb tabs, pop-out)
- **Copy**: the name; **Text** copies the full item text for build gear and listings
- **Stash**: types the name into the stash search
- **Watch / Unwatch**: Market watchlist, when Market is on and poe.ninja prices it

A row's own menu (e.g. "Move to Tier 3" in the filter's price moves) keeps its entries under the strip.

## How
- `layer.ts`: menus can carry an item head; `offerMenuItem(e, head)` lets a right-click offer its item to whichever menu that same click opens.
- `v-item-tip` offers its item in the capture phase and opens the item menu alone if nothing else did, so all 22 screens using it get the menu with no changes.
- Build gear (`GearRowHead`), gems (`GemName`) and trade listings wired by hand.
- Lab gems and scarab flip copy-only row menus now open the item menu; Market drops its Copy/Watch entries (the strip has them); league investments offer the item from anywhere on the row.

## Tests

- `testing/e2e/item-menu.spec.ts`: right-click a Market row, strip + row entries, stash search typed, unwatch, Divine Orb price check shows bulk offers.
