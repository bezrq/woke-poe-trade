_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy's pick C from the mocks: the passive tree drawn with the game's real art, with game-style tooltips.

- The tree is now drawn on a canvas with real icons, frames, cluster backgrounds and masteries. Lines between nodes on an orbit follow the orbit. A tree's new points are ringed and lit green, and respecs red.
- Hovering a node shows its name, stats and state in the game's tooltip at the cursor. Hover reaches at least 14 px, so small passives are easy to hit when zoomed out. Small passives now have stats too.
- Scroll zooms at the cursor, drag pans, and double-click or the header button fits the build. Letting go outside the tree ends the drag.
- The side panel wraps long text instead of cutting it off.

**Art:** the sheets (2.5 MB) load from the addresses in PoB's `sprites.lua` on `web.poecdn.com` when the tree first opens, cached like item art, so the app doesn't grow by them. Offline, nodes fall back to plain dots. `passive-tree.json` grows from 206 KB to 609 KB, still loaded only when opened.

**Cost:** nothing until the tree opens. A full redraw took about 13 ms in a software-rendered test. Hovering only redraws one small layer, and the tree is only redrawn when the view or the trees change.

Checks: unit tests, PoB e2e spec including a hover sweep, `vue-tsc`, eslint, `vite build`, main `tsc`. Captured on Guy's real zizworb build with the real art. Not tried in the game yet.
