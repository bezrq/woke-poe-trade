_Requested by **Guy**_

## Summary

Guy's screenshot showed the Flips note running off the bottom of the screen and sitting off centre from the icon.

- **Cause:** the note was lifted and centred with `transform`, but its `w-enter` opening animation ends on `transform: none` and holds it, which replaced that rule. So every note hung down from its point. Short notes fit, but long ones ran off the screen.
- **Fix:** a note now measures itself once shown and fits to the window, as menus already do. Beside a dock icon it stands above the icon if it fits, else hangs below it. Other notes keep hanging over the panel's bottom edge, centred now, and stay on screen.
- **Card hint:** "Click to outline flips" on the Flips card now matches the size of the text around it.

## Limits

- A note beside an icon whose card is open covers part of the card for its few seconds, as the two want the same space.
- On a vertical dock, a note above or below an icon can cover neighbouring icons.
- Non-dock notes are centred now rather than starting at the panel's centre, so they sit further left than before. Not checked by eye.
- Not checked in the real app.
