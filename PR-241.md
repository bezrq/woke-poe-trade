_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy asked why some mods in the listing tooltip show no roll, such as Eaten Souls on Soul Ascension.

- **Fix:** that cap rolls -10 to +10, per a real trade listing, and any range crossing zero was skipped. The number is now read with its sign, so `+7(-10 to 10)` shows 85%. "5% reduced" counts as below zero. A range crossing zero shows no roll if the line has other numbers or comes from two mods, because neither can be matched safely.
- **Correctly unchanged:** "Eat a Soul ... every 0.5 seconds" comes with 0.5 as both min and max, so it doesn't roll.
- **Landing shot:** the tooltip shot is retaken at full resolution, since the #230 merge kept the old low-res one.
