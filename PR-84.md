### Make dock icons snappy and their cards clickable

- Resting on a dock icon opens its card after 120ms instead of 400ms.
- A left click on an icon opens its card at once, even while the game has focus. Main's global mouse hook sees the click and sends a new `woke-hover-click` event.
- A clickable card takes mouse input after 120ms instead of 350ms.
- The Regex library, Atlas and Mechanics cards had buttons but weren't marked `interactive`, so they couldn't be clicked without the overlay key. They are now.

**Tradeoffs**
- A click on an icon while the game has focus also reaches the game, since the overlay is click-through at that moment.
- Resting on a clickable card turns on the overlay tint and shows any open panels, as the Trade card already does.

**Testing**
- Type checks, 150 unit tests and 17 fake-game specs pass, including a new regex library test. 3 specs were skipped.
- `toolkit.spec` couldn't load on the local PC (ES module import error on `MODULES`), so it relies on CI.
- Not tried in the real game.
