_Requested by **Guy**_

Hovering a row in the trade card (open offers and History) showed the base item, such as Reinforced Greaves for Windscream. Whispers write `Name, Base`, and the comma kept the name from matching the unique, so `itemRefFromText` fell through to its rare fallback.

The comma is now dropped before the name and base are split. Whole-name matching runs first, so names that really hold a comma (Jack, the Axe) still match. Rares named like a unique still get their base.

Tested with a new unit test that fails without the fix, against the 8 offers in the real history file. Typecheck, lint, all renderer tests and the build pass locally. Not hovered in game.
