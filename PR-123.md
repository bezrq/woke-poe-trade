First fix from the loot filter critique (WOKEPOET-34).

**What changed**
- The audit walks the rules for a plain map drop. It used to trust the first rule that listed a base, so Replica and Foulborn rules read as "this base shows".
- Thin markets count for the net and the health list, since a junk price only costs a click. Tier moves still skip them.
- A unique with only variant lines is judged by its best line, never its cheapest. Voices no longer plans a move to hideable at 1c. Tiers use the best real market, and the net uses the best of all. A unique sold per base uses its own base's line.
- The net and health list ignore "Cheapest unique".
- With no prices, the net keeps its last list and warns on the dock, where a save used to drop it silently.
- Your own Hide and "Not for me" hold in game.
- "Show it", "Back to NeverSink's tiers" and "Unlock" now save.

**Tests:** `filter-net.test.ts` checks every strictness level against the real filters, and the e2e no longer presses Save after Show it. Local typecheck, lint, unit tests, build and loot filter e2e pass.
