_Requested by **Guy**_

One merchant with 10 identical rows read SOLID, a 1c bait in a thin market survived, and Sell fast undercut a single outlier: one buyout at 5c under five at 100c suggested 4c.

- Confidence counts at most 2 rows per seller, so SOLID takes at least 4 sellers and OK at least 2. A single seller gets a warning.
- Stash piles count at most 2 rows per seller towards the price. Buyouts all count, because they're real offers.
- With 3 listed, a stash listing under a quarter of the next one is dropped as bait.
- Sell fast skips listings far under the rest, up to a third of them and at most 2. A recent real buyout among them is flagged as possibly worth buying. That example now suggests 97c.
- Under 10c, Sell fast matches the floor rather than cutting a third off it.
- The headline says "Asking around" in place of "Likely sells for", and the rail says "Typical", because every source is an asking price.

Limit: with merchant-only off and the default `api` collapse, the server sends one row per seller, so the wall check still can't see copies.
