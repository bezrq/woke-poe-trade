## Summary

Adds a Scarab flip panel in the Economy group. It answers whether vendoring cheap scarabs pays right now. Hovering its dock icon shows the verdict.

- **Buy list:** scarabs worth vendoring, ranked by the profit the day's trading could supply. Prices with under 1c traded a day are hidden by default.
- **Odds:** the chance of each price range and its share of the value, the biggest sources of value, and how much rests on 200c+ results.
- **Rare & cheap:** scarabs dropping under 0.15% of the time that cost under a third of a typical rare one. They also get a star in the buy list. Guy added this one, since they may rise later.
- **Options:** sell discount, and rerolling results worth under a third of a recipe.

## How it calculates

Recipe, from the PoE Wiki: any 3 scarabs give a random scarab of a different type from those used, respecting drop weights. Each row's profit removes the input from the pool. Only recipes using 3 of the same scarab are modelled.

Weights are a 33,333-roll 3.27 player sample, linked from the wiki. 15 live scarabs missing from it use the average of their wiki rarity tier. 6 with neither are left out and listed in the footer tooltip. The game is on 3.29, so weights may have drifted.

Prices and volume come from poe.ninja's exchange feed, fetched while the panel is placed, at most every 20 minutes. If that fails, the panel uses the dense prices without volume. Reading `volumePrimaryValue` as chaos traded in 24 hours is my interpretation, as poe.ninja doesn't document it.
