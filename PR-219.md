_Requested by **Guy**_

The search button on a Gear tab row now opens the price check on that item, instead of the trade site for uniques only.

- `pob-builds/item-text.ts` writes PoB's item as advanced game item text, so the price check's own parser and filters run: pseudo totals for resistances and life, influences, links, item level.
- `parseItemText` resolves `(41-55)` ranges using PoB's inline `{range:}` or `<ModRange>`, else the middle, as PoB does. Item tooltips now show those values too.
- The button only shows on the English client, since PoB's text is English.

Checked on Guy's real builds: 593 of 600 distinct gear items parse. The 7 that don't are old or removed uniques, or missing a base, and show the price check's normal error. 130 items keep some lines the parser doesn't know, such as PoB notes, and those are just left out of filters.

Tests: new unit tests, a new e2e test, the existing PoB e2e spec, `vue-tsc`, eslint. Not tried in the game yet.
