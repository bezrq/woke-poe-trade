_Requested by **Guy**_

Bulk goods got a raw table with no verdict, so a cheap offer with 3 in stock looked like the price of your 40.

- A verdict card for scarabs, essences and other bulk items: the per-item asking price, the total for your stack, a confidence badge, and Sell fast and Patient prices.
- It counts sellers holding enough for your stack, and says how many offers it left out. If fewer than 3 of the sellers shown hold that much, smaller offers count, it says so, and it hides the stack total.
- Each seller counts once, at their best offer, and your own offers and ones that can't fill a trade are ignored.
- A lone offer far under the rest is flagged as possibly worth buying and doesn't set Sell fast.

It follows the Chaos or Divine tab you have open, and only shows in the improved view. The prices are text rather than copy buttons, since a bulk note isn't always a single amount.

From the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/T7Ty4PqX9C1iKtLGvdqovp), without the poe.ninja footer, because the existing poe.ninja line stays above it.

Limit: it reads the 20 cheapest offers the search returns, unless the stock filter is on, so a seller with a large stock further up isn't seen.

Checks: adversarial review, then fixes for what it found. New tests on the rules, full unit suite, typecheck, lint and build. Offers in the tests are made up, and I didn't open the price check window.
