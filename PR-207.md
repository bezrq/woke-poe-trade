_Requested by **Guy**_

## Summary

Guy wanted the stash outlines to show what a whole stack is worth, not only per scarab. He picked layout B: per 1 on top, stack total under it. Colours stay graded per 1.

- **Stack counts:** the reader now reads the number in each filled slot, by matching the game's digit shapes in main, with no OCR worker.
- **Total:** per item profit times the count, in full sets of 3, as the vendor takes threes. A stack with no full set shows no total.
- **Setting:** "Show stack total on stash outlines" in the Flips settings, on by default.

## Limits

- Counts read only at a 1440p game window. Any other size shows no total, rather than guess.
- The digit shapes were cut from the same shots they are tested on, so a different stash or font setting is untested. A wrong read would show a wrong total with no warning.
- Full sets of 3 was my default. Guy did not object, but did not confirm it either.
- A digit that is covered but still scores below 0.6 ends the number early, so a tooltip or cursor over the last digit can show too low a total. A partly covered digit scoring between 0.6 and 0.85 gives no total.
- The empty slot "Ritual Scarab of Corpses" on the Breach layout reads as filled, as before, so it still gets an outline. It gets no total.
- The count is read for every filled slot even with totals off. It adds little to the read.
- The total line can touch the bottom of a stack count at 1440p. It stays readable in a captured shot, and text scale above 100% would cover more.
- Not checked in the real app.

## Testing

- `testing/vision/stash-read.test.ts`: every count in 3 real shots against values checked by eye. The 2 stashes hold 220 filled slots, and the Faustus shot repeats the first. The read still takes about 70 ms.
- `renderer/test/woke/stash-marks.test.ts`: full sets, no count, no full set, thousands, losses.
- `testing/e2e/scarab-flip.spec.ts`: total line on by default, gone when switched off. 10 passed. One slot crop was also looked at by eye at 2560x1440.
- Unit tests, type checks and lint pass locally. The rest of the fake-game suite was not run.
