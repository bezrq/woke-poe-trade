_Requested by **Guy**_

Most CI runs take about 6.5 minutes, but 2 of the last 10 took 15 to 16. In the one checked, an Ubuntu mirror took 8.5 minutes to serve 22 MB during the Chromium install. This keeps those `.deb` files in the Actions cache and puts them back before installing, so apt only downloads what changed.

The first run fills the cache. To check it works, look at the "Packages from the cache" line and the install time on a later run. WOKEPOET-112.
