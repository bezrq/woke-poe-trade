## Summary

Each ticked number filter gets a column in the price check listings, read from what the trade site already sends. It makes no extra requests.

```diff
 <TradeListing>
   thead
-    Price | count | ilvl | Listed
+    Price | count | ilvl if ticked | ES | Res | Chaos | Life | +N | Listed
   tbody
+    Yours (sticky): the checked item's numbers
     listing rows
+      theirs better: red. Yours better: green. Flips where lower is better.
```

```text
listingStatValue (woke/price-insight/stat-columns.ts)
  defences, dps     item.extended (20% quality, what the filters search)
  block, aps, crit  item.properties
  totals            sum the mods using the filter's pseudo rule
  single mods       parse the mod text, match stat and domain
```

- Columns are capped by window width, 4 at the default. The rest go behind a "+N" header.
- Listings at one price only merge when their numbers match, so no row hides a number.
- `dropWalls` now adds up a seller's copies at one price across rows, so ticking a stat can't change the verdict.
- Item level only gets a column when ticked, and the count bar gives way to stat columns.
