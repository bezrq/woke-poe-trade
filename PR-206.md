_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy's pick from the mocks: the Alt view layout with tier tags on the left, roll quality % on the right, and a bottom row of numbers. Plus the game's real header art, which he asked for after.

- **Listing tooltip:** weapon lines with damage colours, requirements, prefix/suffix/crafted headings, `P1`-style tags, rolls like `136(120-139)%`, and a roll quality %. The bottom row shows pDPS, eDPS and DPS, or defences, marked "at 20% quality" until the item has that quality. No extra requests: the trade site sends all of it.
- **Roll quality:** where the roll sits in its range, rounded down so 100% means perfect. Lines with several rolls average them. Game data's "better" direction flips it for downsides (more `-% to all Resistances` scores lower). A line whose numbers don't clearly match its range shows no roll instead of a wrong one.
- **Header art:** item, lookup and currency tooltips use the game's header and divider images, bundled from Sidekick's MIT repo (credited). The art itself is GGG's. Gem and divination card tooltips keep the colour band.

Adversarial review findings fixed: downside rolls reading as perfect, a fixed number mistaken for the roll, flask mods sitting under a suffix heading, class requirement order, `%0` templates, multi-line mods.
