_Requested by **Guy**_

An Onyx Amulet with T1 crit multi and T1 spell damage searched with no mods ticked, matched any rare amulet, and could read "Not worth listing".

- T1 and T2 mods now start ticked on rares, best first, up to 4. Default totals give way to them, and properties like DPS don't count towards the 4. Veiled rares are unchanged.
- Cluster jewels tick their notables and not their filler rolls.
- A rare searched with no mods gets a "NO MODS" note in place of a listings price, and that search stays out of the price log. A poeprices estimate still shows. Rares with nothing to tick, and searches narrowed by links, quality, item level or influence, still get a price.
- A rare searched without one of its top mods shows a price but never "Not worth listing".

Trade-off: a rare with several top mods now starts on a narrower search, so its first result may have fewer listings. Unticking a mod or Widen search brings them back.

Checks: adversarial review, then fixes for what it found. New tests on parsed items, full unit suite, typecheck, lint and build. Some test items are hand-typed. I didn't open the price check window, so the note's look is unchecked.
