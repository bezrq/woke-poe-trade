## What

Guy picked option B from the [mocks](https://claude.ai/artifact/7nD6LFSbd1E3Z73EMhth85), with editable numbers and no test box. The editor never showed NeverSink's stack sizes, and nothing could change them.

- Every tier with a stack size gets a number field on its row, in all 17 groups that use them. Typing redraws the bar as you go.
- Groups whose tiers differ get a bar above the rows, on a log scale, in the tiers' colours. Drag a line to move it; it stops at its neighbours. Arrow keys work too.
- Gold's piles get the same bar. Its bands use the text colour, because its tiers are all on black.
- A warning shows when your number puts a tier behind another one.
- Changes are stored as a new `stack` edit, so they survive strictness changes and NeverSink updates, show in Changes and undo like the rest.

Not in this PR: adding a stack size to a tier that has none, such as General currency. That was option C, which Guy didn't pick.
