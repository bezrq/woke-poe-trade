_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy's pick from the Trades offers mocks (WOKEPOET-74): one-line rows, the next step button on hover, click to open all the options, a setting for the row style, and dismissed offers kept in a history.

- **One-line rows:** buyer, item, price and age. A green dot means in your hideout, a red dot means offline.
- **Click to open and close:** the top offer can now be closed. Once one is closed they stay closed until you open one, and the next offer after an empty queue opens by itself.
- **Offer rows setting:** Settings > Trade assistant: next step on hover (default), next step always shown, or two lines.
- **History:** offers closed without a trade are saved for good in `<userData>/woke-offer-history/offers.json`, including while reopened, so a restart can't lose them. An expandable History section under the offers lists them, with Reopen on each. Reopen puts the offer back at the step it reached. Traded offers stay in the Log tab.
- **After a restart:** the Client.txt replay no longer brings back offers you closed or marked traded, and offers reopened in the last 30 minutes come back open.
- **Keep the offer bubble setting:** the bubble stays until no offers are open by default. A set time instead (5 s, 30 s, 1 min, 5 min) counts from the last whisper or reply and keeps the icon that long too. When the offer the bubble names closes, the next open one takes over.
- **Fixes:** ✓ and ✕ no longer wrap or look squashed (two button rows). Unique art now finds names with a comma, such as "Doedre's Damning, Paua Ring". A toast anchored to a hidden window no longer lands half off screen at the top left.
- **e2e harness:** `overlay.log` now waits for the log watcher to open Client.txt. Lines written before that were never read live, which is what made log tests right after boot fail now and then, on master too.

**Known limits:** the history file has no size cap. With two Trades widgets, each saves its own copy of a closed offer.

**Checks run locally:** `vue-tsc`, main `tsc`, eslint on the changed files and all unit tests (before the last commit, which only touches tests), the renderer build, and `e2e/trade.spec.ts` (10 tests, 6 runs in a row with no failures).
