_Requested by **Guy**_

Guy's pick from the Trades offers mocks (WOKEPOET-74): one-line rows, the next step button on hover, click to open all the options, a setting for the row style, and dismissed offers kept in a history.

- **One-line rows:** buyer, item, price and age. A green dot means in your hideout, a red dot means offline.
- **Click to open and close:** the top offer can now be closed. Once one is closed they stay closed until you open one, and the next offer after an empty queue opens by itself.
- **Row style setting:** Settings > Trade assistant > Offer rows: next step on hover (default), next step always shown, or two lines.
- **History:** offers closed without a trade are saved for good in `<userData>/woke-offer-history/offers.json`. An expandable History section under the offers lists them, with Reopen on each. Reopen puts the offer back at the step it reached. Traded offers stay in the Log tab.
- **After a restart:** the Client.txt replay no longer brings back offers you already closed.
- **Fixes:** ✓ and ✕ no longer wrap or look squashed (two button rows). Unique art now finds names with a comma, such as "Doedre's Damning, Paua Ring". A toast anchored to the hidden Trades window no longer lands half off screen at the top left.
