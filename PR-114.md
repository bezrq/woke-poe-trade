_Requested by **Guy**_

Hovering a dock card opened the whole overlay, and clicks on dock icons also went to the game.

**Cause:** the card's hover zone called `assertOverlayActive`, which tells the renderer the overlay is open. Every shown widget, the widget menu and the dimmed background came up. Icon zones never took the mouse, so clicks passed through.

**Fix**
- While the mouse is over an icon or card, the window takes clicks and the game keeps focus and keys.
- A click enters card mode: the window has focus, but the renderer still sees the overlay as closed. The overlay key upgrades it to the real overlay, and leaving the zone doesn't close that.
- It doesn't catch the mouse while a button is held or the game isn't in front.
- Card buttons that only showed with the overlay open (Atlas copy and save, Mechanics prev and next) now always show. The `interactive` prop is gone, since every card takes clicks.

**Checks:** typecheck, lint, 1671 unit tests and build pass. Two adversarial reviews ran, and their findings are fixed. Not tried in the game yet; the first check should be whether PoE still registers a click on an icon while it keeps focus.
