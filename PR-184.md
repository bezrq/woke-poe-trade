## What

The Rules tab's flat list of about 150 groups becomes a drilldown, as Guy picked from the mocks ("maybe C but group them further ... drilldowns").

- **Categories, chapters, groups**, with a breadcrumb. Categories come first with their icons and counts. NeverSink's 50 chapters read as about 25, with shorter names. Categories under 8 groups skip the chapter level.
- **Filter box** that reaches every level and says where a match lives.
- **Endgame / Campaign / Both** switch, saved. Your own rules show in all three.
- **"Only what I changed"** chip, plus change counts on each level.
- **Pins**, shown at the top.
- **Keys**: Up/Down walk the rows, Left/Backspace go up, Right goes in.

Only how the list reads changes. Rules stay in the file's order.

Also: the play button on a game sound now says why it can't play here, instead of doing nothing.
