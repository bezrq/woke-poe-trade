### Settings window: centre sidebar icons and make the panel more opaque

- **Icons:** Font Awesome's `display` on `.fas` was beating `WIcon`'s centring grid, so the glyph sat in the top left of its box. This also corrects every other place `WIcon` draws a Font Awesome icon.
- **Opacity:** the window used the translucent `--w-panel` (0.86 to 0.96 alpha by theme, with no setting). It now uses a new solid `--w-panel-solid` for each theme. In-game panels stay translucent.

Checked headless in hybrid, modern and native with before/after screenshots. Not checked over the real game or in the packaged app.
