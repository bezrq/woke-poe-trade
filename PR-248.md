## Why
Someone asked why the stash reader hardcodes `REF_HEIGHT = 1440`. The window size already comes from the game window on every read, and the Scarab tab was already scaled. But stack counts, and the Delirium, Delve, Essence and Blight tabs, only worked when the window was exactly 1440 tall.

## What changed (`main/src/woke/stash-read.ts`)
- The four other tabs: the anchor pictures, search area, slot positions and slot size are scaled from 1440p, the same way the Scarab tab is. When scaled, the match bar is 0.85 instead of 0.93, because true matches score about 0.9 and the best wrong spot about 0.55.
- Counts: each count's corner is resampled to 1440p, area averaged for a bigger window and interpolated for a smaller one. Then the existing digit matcher runs on it. Two things make that reliable:
  - Each digit also has a "softened" version, sent to the window size and back, and the better of the two scores counts. The bar for spotting a white stroke drops with it.
  - It tries 3×3 sub-pixel offsets and keeps the longest number, then the surest one. A missed digit gives a shorter read, so the longest read is the full count.
- Below about 1000p (scale < 0.7), no counts are read rather than guessed, and the four other tabs report not found, since they'd show every slot as empty.
- The Flips hints and the landing page now say "1080p or taller" instead of "1440p".

## Tests
`testing/vision/stash-read.test.ts`: the real 1440p screenshots, resized to 1080, 1200, 1600 and 2160, read every count correctly on every tab. A local sweep from 1008p to 2880p showed no wrong counts. A 720p window reads no counts and finds none of the other tabs. A 2160p read stays under the time budget, about 0.4 s compared with 0.13 s at 1440p. No false finds on any non-stash fixture at 720 to 2160.

Caveat: these are resized 1440p screenshots. The game's own rendering at 1080p may differ slightly, so real screenshots at other sizes would be a good follow-up fixture.
