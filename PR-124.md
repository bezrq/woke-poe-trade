## What changed

The wiki, poedb and Craft of Exile item hotkeys and item info buttons used to throw you out to your real browser. They now open a panel in the overlay beside the item.

- **Tabs:** Wiki, poedb and Craft of Exile for the same item. A tab's key switches to it without hovering the item again, and tabs keep their place when you flip between them.
- **Pop-out:** the current tab's key or the header icon sends the page you're on to your real browser.
- **Bookmarks:** a star saves the page, and a Bookmarks tab lists saved pages to filter, open or remove. An optional new hotkey opens the list with no item hovered.
- **Nothing stays over the game:** Esc or the close button returns to the game and drops the page.

Mocks: https://claude.ai/artifact/8Zb1qAHrRVhB8FaAurrCAn

## Notes

- With the overlay focused, the game's hotkeys are off, so main passes the panel's keys back over IPC while the panel is open.
- A panel key needs Ctrl or Alt, or must be an F-key. Anything else would be taken from what you type into the page, so it doesn't work inside the panel; the header icon still pops out.
- Bookmarks are one flat list. The mock's groups were left out.
- This also fixes three-modifier keys in the overlay's key handling.
- Like the rest of the config, a new bookmark is saved when the panel closes, so quitting or crashing with the panel open loses it.
- The map verdict card can now be resized too, but its centred anchor makes the bottom edge move at half speed.

## Testing

- Typecheck, lint, unit tests (1880) and both builds pass locally.
- On the dev overlay over its debug port: tabs, pop-out icon, star, bookmark list, opening a bookmark, and the no-item view.
- Not tested: real key presses inside the panel (tab switch, pop-out key, Esc), or the panel's size being saved. Both need a real keyboard and mouse in game.
