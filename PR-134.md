_Requested by **Guy**_

Tier items as chips with prices and bulk changes (option Y from the mock, Guy's pick).

- Each chip shows today's poe.ninja price, coloured like the hidden items list: gold at or above the safety net, plain in between, red under 5c. Chips sort most valuable first. Groups with no prices leave the price out.
- A click picks a chip, and shift-click picks a run. A bar then offers Move to (each tier drawn in its own look), Hide, Own look and Take off for all of them, with one Undo.
- Own look points the style controls at the picked items, with a note saying so and a way back to the whole tier.
- Dragging moves everything picked. Right-click "Move to" on a picked chip does too.

**A note on dragging:** it passes in the e2e spec. Guy asked if it's possible, so it may not work in the real overlay. I couldn't test that here.

**Tests:** unit tests for picking and sorting, and an e2e spec for prices, the bar, moving and hiding together, checked in the saved file.
