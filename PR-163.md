## Summary

The Scarab flip panel becomes **Flips**, with one tab per family. Guy picked the tabbed layout from [the mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/HZ3QdC95eYt4ZZY6CPyygs).

| Tab | Flips priced |
|---|---|
| Scarabs | Vendor 3 for 1 |
| Catalysts | Vendor 3 for 1, Harvest reroll |
| Delirium | Harvest reroll |
| Fossils | Harvest reroll |
| Essences | Harvest reroll (Deafening), vendor up a tier |
| Oils | Vendor up a tier |
| Runegrafts | Vendor 3 for 1, flagged low confidence |

Each tab with weights keeps the Buy list and Odds views. Rare & cheap stays on Scarabs and adds Fossils. The dock card now shows the best flip across all families, leaving out low-confidence ones.

Placed panels carry over: the module id and widget type stay `scarab-flip` and `woke-scarab-flip`, and a saved "Scarab flip" title is renamed.

## How it calculates

- **Rules:** from the PoE Wiki. Vendor catalysts and runegrafts say only "random", so the input can come back. Harvest and the scarab recipe exclude it.
- **Harvest cost:** 30 lifeforce per item, in the craft's colour, at the lifeforce's live price. The wiki says the cost is proportional to stack size, and reading its 30 as per item is my interpretation, not yet checked in game. It is an editable field on Harvest tabs.
- **Weights:** PoeData.dev's Bayesian calculations (3.19 to 3.27 depending on family). The existing 3.27 sample stays for scarabs.
- **Missing prices:** items poe.ninja doesn't price sit out of the pool. If that is 10% or more of a family's sampled weight, the panel warns that the average may be too high. Today that applies to delirium orbs, at 24%.
- **Volume:** one exchange feed per poe.ninja type, 7 requests at most every 20 minutes while the panel is placed. A family whose feed fails still works, without volume.

Not priced: Harvest oil rerolls (no public colour weights) and essence rerolls below Deafening.
