Test-only, no app changes. Hardens the tests that fail under full-suite load and pass alone, as #149 did.

## What changes

- **Harness `boot`:** waits for `gameLogWatcher.restart` to finish, plus a moment for `watchFile`'s first look. A line written before that counts as old and is never seen. This is a likely cause of specs that log one area line and wait for a dock icon. A review reached that by reading the code; I didn't reproduce it directly.
- **`game-log-watcher.test.ts`** (mine, from #133): `until` waits 20 s instead of 5 s, and new helper `appendUntilSeen` writes again until the line arrives, because `watchFile` misses a write before its baseline.
- **`loot-filter.spec.ts` "prices: a hidden valuable unique…":** gets `test.slow()`, since it's a long walk through the editor. I couldn't find a `boundingBox` call in that test, so this assumes the reported timeout is the test running out of time.
- **Dock hovers:** `boundingBox` waits up to 20 s for the icon.
