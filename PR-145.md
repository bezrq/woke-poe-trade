_Requested by **Guy**_

Two reports from Guy on the new item tooltip:

- **"Next to the window instead of next to the cursor, hard to read."** Item tooltips now follow the cursor, below and to its right, flipping at the screen's edges. A tooltip too big for either side slides along the edge. Gem names in the build panel still open above the name, as in the game.
- **"Why this show like a white item"** for a base in the filter's unique tiers. It now shows the best unique on that base in full, then every unique on the base with its price and grade (`worth it`, `some value`, `trash`), using the same breakdown and safety net line as the Prices tab. With no prices yet, it shows just the list.

This replaces #142's approach at the same call sites.
