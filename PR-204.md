_Requested by **Guy**_

## Summary

Stash outlines didn't work on Guy's other stash, whose Scarab tab has an extra Breach button. That moved the SCARAB text the reader used as its marker, so it reported "can't read this tab". The same stash also holds different scarabs in some slots.

- **Marker:** now the row symbols at the left of the tab, which stay put. On all 47 of Guy's screenshots they scored 0.99 or more on the 3 Scarab tab shots checked (0028, 0035, 0040) and 0.48 or less elsewhere.
- **Layouts:** each stash layout has its own slot table, chosen by where the SCARAB text sits. An unknown layout draws nothing.
- **Filled slots:** decided by the blue backing at a slot's corners rather than brightness, which is what the review asked about for screen brightness, and which dark items need.
- **Search area:** left half of the screen, top 60%, which keeps the read at about 65 ms in tests, not counting the screenshot.

## Limits

- 3 filled slots on the Breach-button layout have no name in the table, as their icons didn't match, so they get no outline. 105 of its 116 named slots read as filled in that screenshot.
- Slot names are spot-checked by eye. Not checked in the real app.

## Testing

- `testing/vision/stash-read.test.ts` adds the Breach-button screenshot: 8 passed.
- Unit tests, type checks and the flips fake-game tests pass locally; the rest of the fake-game suite was not run.
