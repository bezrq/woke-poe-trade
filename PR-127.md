_Requested by **Guy**_

After a 429 the client tracked only the rate limit window, so a search went out after 12 s of a 60 s penalty, and repeat violations can extend it.

- Reads `Retry-After` and the penalty in `x-rate-limit-*-state`, per policy, and assumes 60 s if a 429 names neither
- Holds search, exchange and fetch requests until the penalty ends
- Retry shows a countdown and stays off until then, on item and bulk results
- Measure and Widen search wait it out, and the free search count shows 0

Checks: 9 new tests with simulated headers, full unit suite, typecheck and lint. Not tested against a real 429.
